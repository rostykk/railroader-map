Which cars the game sends, and how many touch the zone

Every industry on the map, with the car models the game can bill to it, the odds of each model for your locomotive roster, and how many cars fit fully inside or just touch the loading zone.

1. A random model of the right type

An order takes every car model matching the industry's car type, sorts them by empty weight, and picks one on a bell curve. PrefabStoreExtensions.Random

2. Your locomotives tilt it

The curve's centre follows the median rated tractive effort of all your locomotives: small engines mean light, short cars; big power means heavy, long ones. TrainController.InferSizePreference

3. Team tracks pick a load first

A team track picks one load from its list at random, then a car of that load's type, and keeps at most its target number of cars on order. TeamTrack.OrderCars

4. Touching the zone is enough

A car counts as at the industry if any part of it overlaps the loading zone, so cars can stick out onto the clear track. TrainController.GetCarsOnSpan

Interchanges

Every car an industry orders arrives at an interchange first. The game rotates each industry's orders among all the interchanges you have open and can reach, a few cars at a time, so every open interchange gets a share of every industry's cars. A loaded car going back off-line returns to the interchange it came from.

Each delivery fills an interchange up to 70% of (track length ÷ 50 ft) cars, minus the cars already standing there. Orders over that wait for the next delivery.

Buy a load: bring your own empty car of the right type, billed to one of these customers. At the next delivery the game fills it, sends it off-line and brings the same car back loaded about a day later. No random cars are involved.

Maintenance of Way deliveries

Delivery milestones (diesel stands, water columns, signals, engine service sites) order their own cars, free of charge, from the interchange. Each car is picked like any industry's: a random model of the right type, leaning on your roster size. All the cars of a milestone are ordered at once.

Track construction milestones (new track, wyes, bridges) order nothing: you bring the materials in your own work cars, so their size is whatever you own.

Reading the numbers

Fully inside, ≥ 20% in and Touching show the count for the typical car of your class, with the range from the longest to the shortest likely model (5% chance or more). All three keep every car on clear track, from the bumper or the switch's fouling point, so no car stands on a switch.

≥ 20% in is the rule the schematic uses: a car counts when at least a fifth of it stands in the zone. The game itself serves a car that only touches the zone, but a few inches of overlap is not something to plan around. The schematic label (map) uses the typical car of the industry's type, about 41 ft for boxcars, the same for every roster.

For mines and quarries the row shows the empties track; the loaded cars go to the mine's loads tracks, shown under the row.

Room at once for a Maintenance of Way site uses the same rule as the schematic (at least 20% of each car in the zone, whole car on clear track) with the average car of the milestone for your roster. When it is smaller than the cars ordered, deliver in batches.

Not covered: cars you own and send yourself (any model you bought), and cars returning from off-line trips (they keep their model).
